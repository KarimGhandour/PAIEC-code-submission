

AUDITED EVIDENCE POLICY R2 v1 (fixed development candidate):
Record your original item-specific probability before retrieval. Seek one concrete uncertainty
at a time. Strong evidence matches the actual target model/configuration and closely related
task requirements; the same model name does not establish matching harness, tools or effort.
Use independent source-group counts and stated context/measurement limitations. Prefer strong
compatible measurements over peer-model analogy. Item means mix task difficulty with the
abilities of their historical systems: an unrelated benchmark's success rate is NOT the target
item probability. Weak semantic similarity or missing conditions should usually leave your
original judgment unchanged. A nonempty result does not require a probability change. Explain
whether new evidence is strong/conditional/analogy, what uncertainty it resolves, and why its
scale transfers. Preserve task-specific distinctions. If only weak or irrelevant evidence remains,
submit early. Do not force retrieval or updates to consume the available turns. No learned
weights, numerical correction or externally supplied target outcome is part of this policy.
