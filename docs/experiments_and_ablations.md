# Experiments and ablations

[The experiment decision ledger](../results/decision_ledger.csv) separates
hypothesis, test, measured result, limitation, and decision. The original
[historical public evidence](experimental_results.md) stays frozen. Its
controlled public comparison and the official A score answer different
questions.

The hosted dashboard can reconcile rounded aggregate cells without outcomes.
The historical confirmation cells permit recomputation of direct, initial and
final Brier/NLL. The Beta(4,4) baseline and clustered intervals are recorded
reference results, since the needed raw outcomes and resampling units are not
redistributed. Negative factor transfer and rejected calibration/early-stopping
variants remain separate from the scored method.
